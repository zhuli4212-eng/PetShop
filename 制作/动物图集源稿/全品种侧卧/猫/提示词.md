# 猫类侧卧图集生成提示词

四个品种各调用一次内置 ImageGen。每次调用均将以下两张本地 PNG 按列出的顺序传入 `referenced_image_paths`，并设置 `transparent_background` 为 `true`。下列英文文本为各次调用的完整 `prompt`。

## 英国短毛猫

1. 参考图一为[british.png](../../british.png)，提供品种、花色、公母排列与像素风格。
2. 参考图二为[british-coat01_female-rest.png](../../../../PetShop/assets/resources/pets/british-coat01_female-rest.png)，提供闭眼、贴地的侧卧姿态。

生成母图为 [british-atlas.png](british-atlas.png)。

```text
EDIT standing reference #1 to make the final real side-lying prone sprites for the same animals in this pixel art game. Ref #2 is the EXACT target pose: cheek fully rests against floor, LEFT-facing head tilted down, eyes wholly shut (no visible pupils), shoulder and hips lie on their side, slack paws, tail on ground trailing to right. NO sitting, curled ball, loaf, standing or head held above floor. Gentle non-graphic depiction. Match source #1 colors, coat marking identity, moderate outlines, cozy cluster-based pixel art style and detailed legible distinguishing features. Breed British Shorthair, short plush dense coat, compact torso, short rounded ears and cheeks. MALE is slightly larger/fuller cheeks than FEMALE within each coat matching ref1; neither wears accessories. Strict regular atlas. Four columns x three rows. Row 1: blue-grey solid male | EMPTY (the blue-grey female is already finished; keep this exact slot transparent) | cream-white male | cream-white female. Row 2: black tuxedo white chest+feet male | tuxedo female | shaded golden male | shaded golden female. Row 3: silver tiger tabby male | silver tiger tabby female | solid chocolate brown male | solid chocolate brown female. Every cell SAME SIZE in a uniform aligned grid, ONE complete isolated wide horizontal left-facing full side-lying cat centered per specified cell, visible boundaries only transparent gutters. All nonempty cell sprites similar footprint, uniform art scale. Large clear transparent margins between animals so all parts crop separately for game sprites. RGBA TRANSPARENT cutout background, no ground shadows or floor, no dividers, text, labels, counters, frames, vignettes, or UI. Only the closed-eyed animals. Precisely match identities and short- or long-fur silhouettes in original, warm dark brown pixel lines rather than stark black. Canvas wide landscape.
```
