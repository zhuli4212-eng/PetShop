# 兔类侧卧图制作记录

四张 `*-source.png` 是内置 ImageGen 生成的透明母稿。每张图只画一种兔。正式版新增 40 个品种、花色与性别组合，用于此前没有专用图的个体。原色兔 01 以及安哥拉兔 01、02、06 沿用既有的公母共用侧卧图。

## 图像参考及排列

- 第一张输入是该品种各行的成年图。取自 [正式动物资源](../../../../PetShop/assets/resources/pets)，文件名为 `<breed>-coatNN_male.png` 和 `<breed>-coatNN_female.png`。按下表的顺序，自上向下排列。左列公，右列母，两列都朝左。制作输入时在透明画布的 240×119 格子里居中放置成年图的可见轮廓。
- 第二张输入的三个侧卧姿势从左至右为 [原色兔](../../../../美术确认稿/已故/brown-dead.png)、[白色安哥拉](../../../../美术确认稿/已故/angora-dead.png)、[花斑兔](../../../../美术确认稿/已故/spot-dead.png)。这些图提供闭眼、头贴地、耳向后收及游戏像素风格的参考。

## ImageGen 提示词模板

每个品种单独生成一张透明图集。替换 `ROWS`、`COUNT` 和 `BREED_AND_COATS`，最后一项取上表的品种、行序、毛色及轮廓描述。两张参考图的含义保持一致。

```text
Game sprite atlas on genuine transparent background. Image 1 is the identity and exact ordered reference grid, image 2 is a reference for the already approved closed-eye posture and pixel rendering.
Draw a NEW sheet of exactly COUNT individual BREED_AND_COATS.
EXACT GRID: ROWS perfectly spaced horizontal rows, TWO equal columns. Each regular cell contains exactly one full rabbit, centred with a transparent margin. No text, no background, no ground or painted ground shadow, no lines/grid and no borders.
Every animal is recumbent in side profile facing LEFT like the right and middle figures in reference 2. Low still side-lying posture, shoulder and cheek on the ground, hindquarters resting, front and hind feet relaxed, chin down and eye fully closed into a single thin curved eyelid with NO white pupil glint. No sitting or standing animals, all four feet natural.
Keep the source animal's individual fur and sex appearance: left column a slightly bigger/broader male, right a slightly sleeker female of the same coat, both left facing. Across cells same camera and scale.
Pixel-art sprite style closely match reference outlines, stepped pixels, flat clusters of warm ramps, no painting, no soft shadows, no smooth renders. Use most of each cell width and do not let silhouettes touch cells.
```

## 正式小图导出

从项目根目录运行以下命令。依赖 Pillow、NumPy 和 OpenCV。脚本按品种行列裁出独立侧卧轮廓，以同花色、同性别的成年图校色，输出 96×64 的二值透明 PNG。绘制的最低像素为 y=58，PNG 和对照图相同则保留原文件。

```powershell
python -B "制作/动物图集源稿/全品种侧卧/兔/export_rabbit_rest.py"
```

[40 个外观的成年与侧卧对照](兔类40种外观侧卧对照.png)中，每个品种的每一行都是一种花色，依次展示公兔成年、侧卧以及母兔成年、侧卧。
