# 银虎斑公美国短毛猫独立行走稿

成年与幼年各调用一次内置 ImageGen，分别以该年龄的一张静帧作为唯一参考图，参数 `transparent_background` 为 `true`。下列英文为采用的两张母稿的完整提示词。

## 成年

参考图为 [american-coat01_male.png](../../../../PetShop/assets/resources/pets/american-coat01_male.png)。生成母稿为 [adult-sheet.png](adult-sheet.png)。

```text
Production 2D sprite animator, draw FOUR genuinely DIFFERENT hand-keyed poses in a horizontal left-facing walk cycle for this one specific cat. Use the supplied sitting portrait for identity only. An adult male American Shorthair, full-grown muscular short-furred build, long horizontal BACK and straight balanced tail, modest almond eye, EXACT silver classical broad bullseye black tabby, striped forehead and tail as ref. Use a new drawing per pose, not duplicated / translated / mirrored bodies. The four side-view locomotion silhouettes must read individually: WIDE STRIDE / LIFTED BACK FOOT / NARROW UNDERBELLY CROSS / LIFTED FRONT PAW. Cat faces LEFT, nose LEFT tail RIGHT, throughout. Show the nearer leg of each pair in pale silver with charcoal stripes, far limbs noticeably darker in shadow behind them. Mandatory silhouettes, read individually left to right: (1) front near paw is a long diagonal from shoulder down to the LEFT, touching ground far ahead, near hindleg stretched diagonally BACK RIGHT with toe leaving floor. The dark far legs are inside the stance. (2) The near front paw now supports weight straight below the shoulder. Clearly lift and fold the hind hock; the raised rear paw swings forward into the space under the belly, not touching ground. Far front reaches forward low with elbow slightly flexed. (3) Essential reverse key: the light foreground front leg SLOPES DOWN AND RIGHT from shoulder, NOT left. Its paw is under the center of the belly, farther right than its elbow. The light foreground HIND leg simultaneously angles down and LEFT from hip reaching toward the forepaw. Both light foreground paws collect/cross close together beneath the mid-belly; the silhouette is prominently NARROW. Show far leg advancing just below muzzle in deep shadow, do NOT stick a bright paw out ahead of the nose. Do NOT draw the first wide-leg pose again. (4) Light forepaw curls UP and forward high, with a small visible gap from ground and a bent wrist; dark far forelimb stays planted. The near hindleg is UNDER hip, supporting weight. The legs and paws in frames 1 and 3 should clearly exchange directions; frames 2 and 4 should show opposite lifted paws. Cat walks normally with small vertical weight shift and a slight tail arc. Matching cozy pixel art, strong animal-like proportions, side-on with readable open space underneath chest and belly, detailed stepped outlines. One horizontal 4-key animation sprite sheet, transparent background, generous padding around every cat, identical scale, no frame numbers, letters, floor or cast shadows. Deliver four original drawings and four distinct lower-body silhouettes.
```

## 导出

在此目录运行 `python export_walk.py` 可从两份母稿重新生成 384×96 候选条。脚本选择四个角色连通区域，每个年龄统一缩放倍率、清理透明边、居中，并使每格最底像素落在 y=89。动作、轮廓和关节都来自母稿。
