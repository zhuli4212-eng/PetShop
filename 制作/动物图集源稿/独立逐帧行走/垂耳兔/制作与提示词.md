# 焦糖白花母垂耳兔独立跳跃候选

成年与幼年各自单独生成一张 2×2 的 ImageGen 原稿。每次只输入该年龄的正式静帧，裁去透明留白、四周各加 6 像素再做 6 倍整数最近邻放大，便于查看脸部、耳朵和花斑。成年、幼年使用的静帧分别是 [成年立绘](../../../../PetShop/assets/resources/pets/lop-coat06_female.png) 和 [幼年立绘](../../../../PetShop/assets/resources/pet-young/lop-coat06_female.png)。两张原稿以透明背景生成，均保存在本目录。

## 导出与接触点

[成年母稿](adult-source.png)和[幼年母稿](young-source.png)的四格都按左上、右上、左下、右下读取，得到蓄力、后蹬、腾空、前爪落地。成年整套采用 0.145、幼年整套采用 0.116 的固定等比缩放。每格只裁透明留白、清理透明杂点、等比缩小并平移到 96×96 画布。耳、前爪、后腿和躯干的四种姿势都来自所在母稿的原格。

运行 `python -B "制作/动物图集源稿/独立逐帧行走/垂耳兔/export_frames.py"` 可从仓库根目录重新导出。依赖 Pillow、NumPy 和 OpenCV。[成年候选条](adult-walk.png)和[幼年候选条](young-walk.png)均为 384×96 透明 PNG，横排四个不同帧。重复导出会保留内容相同的文件。

| 核对项 | 成年 | 幼年 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 各帧最低实像素 y | 89、89、81、89 | 89、89、84、89 |
| 后蹬触地点 | 画面右侧的后趾在 y=89 | 画面右侧的后趾在 y=89 |
| 腾空收脚 | 最低处离地线 8 像素 | 最低处离地线 5 像素 |
| 前爪落地→蓄力 | 循环接缝两帧地线重叠在 x=33 | 循环接缝两帧地线在 x=31–41 的范围内有重叠 |

## 成年提示词

```text
An ADULT female miniature lop rabbit: full adult torso and hind haunch, long pendulous cinnamon ears. Real adult rabbit proportions, lean forward into an energetic long hop. The adult standing portrait supplied is the ONLY identity reference.
Draw a real hand-animated 4-POSE pixel sprite model sheet. Reference: use the supplied static illustration ONLY for identity, age, markings and game's art style. Redraw every pose entirely. This is a caramel-and-ivory broken-pattern lop; white blaze, caramel patch surrounds visible dark eye, both long floppy ears caramel, mainly ivory cheeks/chest/belly and irregular tawny patches across the back/rump. Eye open. Clear small dark warm outlines, delicate compact game pixels, flat pixel clusters, no new accessories. True left-facing side profile, nose at left, tail at right in EVERY pose. Clean genuinely transparent background, no floor line, no shadow, no writing/numbers/grid. Each whole rabbit fully visible, with empty gutters between poses. Exactly FOUR poses in a regular 2x2 grid, top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Side-view game walk cycle on a fixed camera; same head SIZE and body mass throughout. Preserve the markings, ear length and anatomy through the poses. Do NOT show four static sitting portraits. Make silhouettes of the legs and ears visibly different, with negative space between paws. Natural rabbit hind legs with two long hock sections; no dog feet, wings or extra limbs. Keep the SAME zoom for ALL FOUR, not automatically rescaled to occupy the same box.
TOP LEFT / phase 1 (compressed gathering pose): hind haunch folded far underneath torso, forepaws stably on the ground under shoulders, chest and chin LOW, knees and long hind feet coiled on ground - short compact silhouette, pendant ears hang down. TOP RIGHT / phase 2 (push-off): near hind leg and hind foot powerfully EXTENDED backward to viewer's RIGHT, rear toes alone contact ground, visibly elongated diagonal from up-left head through pelvis to low-right hind toes. Both forepaws already raised off the ground, floppy ears drag back. BOTTOM LEFT / phase 3 (airborne recoil/apex): body lifted as a whole, space under belly, four paws entirely airborne; fold hind hocks forward under the belly and tuck forepaws in. Head looks left, floppy ears momentarily float back in an unmistakable horizontal curve. BOTTOM RIGHT / phase 4 (landing): ONLY forward reaching forepaws touch ground toward viewer's LEFT; chest lowered over planted forepaws, rump still high at right, hind feet gathered underneath rump and still above line of forepaws. Drooping ears swing forward alongside cheek. This must feed naturally back into compression. The result must read as a repeating animated bound when four cells appear rapidly in that order.
```
